Tavli Master Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Overview

Tavli Master is developed by John Tsinonis. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app.

Information we process

The app does not require an account to play the core game. Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • Game settings, preferences, statistics, and match history stored on your device.
  • A player name and game results used for online multiplayer and leaderboards.
  • Game Center information when you choose to sign in with Game Center.
  • Technical information needed to deliver advertising and maintain online services.

Advertising

Tavli Master uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. Google may process device and advertising information in accordance with its policies. Where required, the app presents consent and privacy choices. You can review available advertising privacy choices from Settings.

Online services

Online matchmaking, friend matches, and leaderboards use third-party hosting services. Information needed to operate those features, such as player names, room codes, match results, and connection information, may be transmitted to those services.

Children

Tavli Master is not directed specifically to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account to play.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences and local game history can be removed by deleting the app or clearing its data. If you need help with information associated with online services, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app and its services change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact pareagamesjt@gmail.com.
