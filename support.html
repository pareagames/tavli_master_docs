Need help?

Email pareagamesjt@gmail.com and include:

  • Your device model and operating-system version
  • The app version and build number
  • The game mode or screen where the issue occurred
  • A screenshot or short description of the problem

We will do our best to respond and help resolve the issue.

Quick help

You can play against the computer without signing in. Online friend matches use a room code, while same-Wi-Fi matches require both devices to be connected to the same local network.
